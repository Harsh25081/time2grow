# Post Formats

## LinkedIn

Use for founders, agencies, SaaS, services, B2B, hiring, launches, lessons, and proof-led business posts.

Structure:

1. Hook: one sharp line.
2. Context: why this matters.
3. Body: 3-5 short paragraphs or bullets.
4. Insight/proof: only if supplied or from Business DNA.
5. CTA: comment, DM, book, visit, try, or learn.
6. Hashtags: 3-5 relevant tags.

Keep lines short. Avoid hype.

## Blog

Use when the output should be longer, educational, SEO-friendly, or website-ready.

Structure:

1. SEO title.
2. Intro.
3. 3-5 sections with headings.
4. Practical examples.
5. Closing CTA.

Keep it readable and factual. Do not invent external data.

## Community Post

Use for WhatsApp groups, Telegram, Facebook groups, communities, and local audience updates.

Structure:

1. Friendly opener.
2. Main message.
3. Offer/details.
4. Simple CTA.
5. Optional short hashtags.

Keep it conversational and direct.
