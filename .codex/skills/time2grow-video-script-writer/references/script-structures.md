# Script Structures

## Direct Ad

Use for offers, lead generation, product promotions, and urgent campaigns.

Flow:

1. Hook/problem.
2. Product/brand entry.
3. Benefit/proof.
4. Offer or reason to act.
5. CTA.

## Story Ad

Use for emotional, family, local-business, showroom, or customer-journey ads.

Flow:

1. Character problem/desire.
2. Tension or relatable moment.
3. Brand/product solution.
4. Result/payoff.
5. CTA.

## Educational Short

Use for coaches, SaaS, services, explainers, and awareness videos.

Flow:

1. Hook question.
2. 3 points or steps.
3. Example.
4. CTA.

## Cartoon/Character Ad

Use for playful local ads, farming, electronics, appliances, kids/family, and light comedy.

Flow:

1. Funny misunderstanding or problem.
2. Character reaction.
3. Product solution.
4. Humorous payoff.
5. CTA.

## Timing Guide

- 15 sec: 3 scenes.
- 30 sec: 4-5 scenes.
- 45 sec: 5-7 scenes.
- 60 sec: 6-8 scenes.
